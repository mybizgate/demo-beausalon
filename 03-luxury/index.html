    The house

    Private suites. Quiet rules.

    Every room is a closed suite with its own shower and dressing area. Female therapists only. No walk-ins, no waiting room noise, no phone calls during your hour.

    When should I arrive?

    Ten minutes before your hour. Your suite is prepared from the minute you booked, and we begin on time so the next guest's hour is protected too.

    Is the atelier hijab-friendly and private?

    Yes. All therapists are women, every suite is fully enclosed, and we provide a private changing area with a lockable door.

    What should I wear or bring?

    Nothing in particular. Robes, slippers, disposable undergarments and a hair wrap are provided. Arrive without makeup for facials if you can.

    Can I reschedule or cancel?

    Free changes up to 24 hours before your hour. Inside 24 hours a 50% ritual fee applies, as the suite was held for you.

    Pregnancy, sensitivities or medical notes?

    Tell us in the reservation note. We adapt pressure, products and positioning, and some treatments have pregnancy-safe alternatives.

    Parking and payment

    Reserved bays at the rear entrance, validated for two hours. We accept cards, DuitNow QR and e-wallets. No payment is taken online on this demo.
